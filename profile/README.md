# Sirius Quantum

**Quantum Data Framework for Physical AI.**

Sirius Quantum is the quantum data framework for Physical AI — an on-premise quantum data relabelling engine, an open simulation network, and open-source quantum datasets and benchmarks spanning quantum chemistry, molecular biology, drug development, materials discovery, and custom-built quantum finance.

## Open source

- [Zilver](https://github.com/Sirius-Quantum/zilver): a distributed quantum simulator for integrated GPUs. Apache 2.0.

## Canis M

**Canis M** is the first molecular model trained on quantum tokens: compact representations of a molecule's electronic structure, read by the model in place of atomic coordinates. Trained on 50 molecules, it is more accurate than the standard approach trained on 16,000.

- Model: [SiriusQuantum/canis-m](https://huggingface.co/SiriusQuantum/canis-m)
- Paper: [Tokenising quantum data for label-efficient training of molecular models](https://chemrxiv.org/doi/full/10.26434/chemrxiv.15009119/v1)
- Announcement: [siriusquantum.com/canis-m](https://siriusquantum.com/canis-m)

## Open datasets

Machine-learning datasets with labels from exact quantum computation, across quantum chemistry, drug discovery, quantum many-body physics and quantitative finance, on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/SiriusQuantum).
